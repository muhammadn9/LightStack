# LightStack Privacy Policy (moved)

The single source for the policy text is now `docs/legal/PRIVACY_POLICY.md`. The app bundles it and shows it in Settings > Privacy Policy. Edit only that file.

It still needs legal review, the bracketed placeholders filled in, and publication at a public https URL. When the URL exists, add the `LSPrivacyPolicyURL` key to Info.plist (see `docs/release/APP_REVIEW_COMPLIANCE.md`). The `PrivacyPolicy.url` helper in `SettingsView.swift` reads it.

See `DATA_INVENTORY.md` for what is collected and `ACCOUNT_DELETION_SETUP.md` for deletion.
