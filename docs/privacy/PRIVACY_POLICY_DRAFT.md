## What we collect

- **Account:** your email address, or the identity Apple or Google provides when you sign in with them.
- **Profile you enter:** display name, age, height, weight, training experience, goals, equipment, exercises to avoid and notes to your coach.
- **Training and nutrition you log:** workouts, sets, personal records, plans, rest days, foods, meals, water, daily notes and targets.
- **Coach conversations** you have with the in-app coach.
- **Camera:** form-check video is analysed on your device and is not uploaded or stored.

We do not use advertising or analytics SDKs and do not sell your data.

## How we use it

To run the app, back up and sync your data across sessions, personalise training and nutrition, and, with your permission, generate AI coaching.

## AI coaching and Google Gemini

If you allow it, your profile stats (age, height, weight, goals, training experience, equipment, notes), recent workouts and chat messages are sent through our server to Google Gemini to generate coaching. Your name and email are not required for this. You can turn this off at any time in Settings; then no AI requests are made.
