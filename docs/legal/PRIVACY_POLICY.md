# Privacy Policy

Effective date: [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Lightstack ("the app", "we", "us") is operated by [LEGAL NAME]. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used and what choices you have. Questions: [CONTACT EMAIL].

## What we collect

- **Account.** Your email address if you sign up with email, or the name and email that Apple or Google share when you use "Continue with Apple" or "Continue with Google". We never see your Apple or Google password. For email accounts we store a password through our sign-in provider; we cannot read it.
- **Profile and body data.** What you enter: display name, age, height, weight, training experience, goals, split days, equipment, exercises to avoid and notes to your coach.
- **Training.** Workouts, exercises, sets (weight, reps, effort), personal records, plans and rest days.
- **Nutrition and weight.** Foods, meals, water, daily notes, calorie and macro targets, and weigh-ins you log.
- **Coach conversations** you have with the in-app coach.
- **Camera and photos.** The camera is used for form recording, rep counting and scanning barcodes and nutrition labels. Photos are used only when you pick or take one for AI meal logging.
- **Notifications.** If you allow them, the app schedules reminders such as rest-timer alerts on your device.

We do not include advertising or analytics tools. We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

## How we use it

To run the app: show your history and progress, build workouts and nutrition plans from your own numbers, and back up and sync your data so you can restore it on a new device. We do not use your data for anything else.

## Where it is stored

Your data is stored on your device and backed up to our database and sign-in service, provided by Supabase. Data is protected in transit, and each account can only read its own rows. No system is perfectly secure.

## AI features and Google Gemini

When you use an AI feature, the app sends the relevant information to our server, which forwards it to Google Gemini to produce the answer. Depending on the feature this can include your profile stats (age, height, weight, goals, training experience, equipment, notes to coach), recent workouts, your chat messages, the text you type to describe a meal, a meal photo you choose to send, and key frames from a form recording. We do not send your email address. Google processes this data under its own terms, including how long it keeps it. Our server does not keep the photos or frames after the request. The app asks for your permission before the first AI request, and no AI request is made until you allow it. You can turn AI features off or on at any time in Settings (Privacy, AI features). When they are off, nothing is sent to Google Gemini and the rest of the app keeps working. Do not put health conditions or other sensitive details in free-text notes you do not want processed.

## Form videos

Form recordings are analysed on your device and stored on your device. Only key frames from a recording you ask the coach to review are sent for AI feedback, as described above. Recordings are not uploaded to our servers.

## Food database and barcodes

Food search text and barcodes you scan are sent to Open Food Facts, a public food database, to look up products. No account identifier is sent with them.

## Other services we use

- **Supabase**: database and sign-in.
- **Google Gemini**: AI features, as above.
- **Open Food Facts**: food lookups, as above.
- **Apple and Google**: if you choose their sign-in.

## Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all the data we hold in the app: Settings, Delete account. This removes your account and your training, nutrition and coaching data from our database, and your data on that device. Deleting the app alone removes only the data stored on your device. If you signed in with Apple, we also ask Apple to revoke the sign-in link. If you cannot use the in-app option, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you signed up with. Backups can take additional time to expire.
